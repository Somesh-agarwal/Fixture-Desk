Reports

Sources

What happened on the most recent automatic update. Updates run about 7am, 6pm and 11pm London time. A failed source keeps its previous data and is tried again on the next update.

Status workflow

Scheduled→ Awaiting Result→ Result Verified→ Summary Published any stage can drop toNeeds Review

A fixture moves to Awaiting Result automatically once its expected finish time passes. It only reaches Result Verified when an official results page confirms it. Postponed, cancelled, abandoned and rescheduled fixtures carry a warning tag and go to Needs Review until a new date is confirmed.

Manual import

Paste CSV or an iCalendar (.ics) feed, or choose a file. Imports are checked for duplicates and conflicting times, then held as Needs Review until you confirm the schedule against the official link.

CSV columns: date,time_london,sport,competition,round,participants,venue,country,regions,official_link. Separate participants with ; and regions with ; (india, uk, global). Times are London local.
